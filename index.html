Our Founder

Alh. Modou Turo Darboe

Alh. Modou Turo Darboe is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of the Vision Development Foundation (VDF).

He is a respected Gambian businessman and philanthropist committed to community development, social justice and poverty reduction.

Under his leadership, VDF was established in 2006 as a charitable non-governmental organization registered in The Gambia.

Sectors

Our Key Priority Sectors

Areas where we create lasting impact

Education

  • Scholarships for students pursuing higher and tertiary education.
  • Sponsorship of Gambian students studying abroad.
  • Provision of computers and other learning materials to educational institutions.
  • Support for digital literacy and improved access to educational resources.

Health & Medical Support

  • Organizing free medical camps.
  • Organizing free surgical camps.
  • Providing general medical checkups.
  • Supporting specialized surgeries.
  • Covering overseas medical treatment costs for patients requiring advanced care.
  • Donating hospital beds to healthcare facilities.
  • Donating washing machines to healthcare facilities.
  • Supporting initiatives aimed at improving public health.

Infrastructure & Community Development

  • Construction and rehabilitation of roads.
  • Construction of bridges to improve connectivity between communities.
  • Construction of mosques and support for the completion of religious facilities.
  • Provision of milling machines to communities and local entrepreneurs to reduce labour and support economic activity.

Water, Sanitation & Irrigation

  • Digging boreholes to provide reliable access to clean water.
  • Provision of irrigation facilities for women gardeners.
  • Improving access to water for rural communities and agricultural activities.

Agriculture & Women's Economic Empowerment

  • Supporting women gardeners across the country.
  • Protecting community gardens from wildlife.
  • Providing essential agricultural and garden inputs.
  • Supporting women whose livelihoods depend on agricultural production.
  • Improving working conditions and productivity for women gardeners.
  • Providing irrigation facilities to help women sustain and expand their gardens.
  • Supporting rural livelihoods, self-sufficiency, and household income.

Humanitarian Relief & Disaster Response

  • Providing food and essential supplies to victims of floods and heavy rains.
  • Distributing mattresses and other necessities to disaster-affected families.
  • Providing emergency assistance to vulnerable communities during times of disaster.

Religious & Interfaith Support


Ramadan Relief

  • Providing food and sugar to needy families throughout Ramadan.
  • Supporting vulnerable families so they can observe Ramadan with dignity.
  • Distributing fabric during Koriteh/Eid al-Fitr.
  • Donating bulls for sacrifice and distribution among poor families.

Tobaski / Eid al-Adha

  • Providing hundreds of rams to families who cannot afford them.
  • Helping vulnerable Muslim families fulfil the religious obligation of sacrifice.

Christian Community Support

  • Providing rice, sugar, canned drinks, and financial assistance to Christian families during Christmas.
  • Supporting communities regardless of religious background.


Interfaith Unity

  • Supporting both Muslim and Christian communities.
  • Sponsoring the construction and completion of mosques.
  • Promoting community cohesion and religious inclusivity through humanitarian support.
