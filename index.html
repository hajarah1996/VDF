Our Founder

Alh. Momodou Turo Darboe

Alh. Momodou Turo Darboe is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of the Vision Development Foundation (VDF).

He is a respected Gambian businessman and philanthropist committed to community development, social justice and poverty reduction.

Under his leadership, VDF was established in 2006 as a charitable non-governmental organization registered in The Gambia.

Mission
